Experience

Where I learned what a claim costs

Nov 2024 to Jun 2025

Bachelor thesis researcher

Fraunhofer Institute for Production Technology (IPT)

Characterised a magnetic planar drive printer for polycaprolactone implant scaffolds. I ran a 19-run Box–Behnken study, ANOVA, and clean-room dimensional metrology. The work produced a validated parameter set with 97.14% mean accuracy and showed why the numerical optimum was structurally unusable. The thesis won the VDI Niederrheinischer Bezirksverein university prize.

Aachen, DE

Apr 2024 to Sep 2024

Engineer intern, micro-mechanical fatigue testing

Robert Bosch GmbH. Corporate Research

Tensile and fatigue testing across 84 specimens, including tests run under hydrogen and at the microscale, to quantify embrittlement. Used light microscopy for damage assessment and digital image correlation for strain measurement, and wrote MATLAB scripts to automate the statistical post-processing of test results.
